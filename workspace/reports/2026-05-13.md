**Date:** 2026‑05‑13

## Executive Summary  
The HPC landscape this week is being reshaped by three converging forces: the rapid maturation of quantum‑class accelerators, the mainstreaming of agentic AI workloads, and a fundamental shift in networking architecture toward Ethernet‑based fabrics that rival InfiniBand in latency and scale. IonQ’s new Boulder lab signals that quantum‑ready interconnects and scheduler extensions will become mandatory for future HPC centers, while the DOE’s Genesis Mission AI RFA and the widespread adoption of sub‑200 ms inference pipelines are driving a wave of low‑latency storage and event‑driven scheduling innovations. Finally, the Multipath Reliable Connection (MRC) proposal and Arista’s 1.6 Tb/s roadmap are redefining the fabric design, enabling two‑tier, high‑radix Ethernet topologies that can support >130 k GPUs with fault‑tolerant link loss. These developments collectively demand a re‑evaluation of existing hardware, software, and operational models across the HPC ecosystem.

### 3. Networking Architecture Shifts for Large‑Scale AI  
Multipath Reliable Connection (MRC) is an Ethernet‑based superset of RoCE that introduces 512 × 100 Gb/s ports, 8‑path packet spraying, ECN‑based load‑balancing, packet‑trimming, and SRv6 static routing, enabling a two‑tier fabric that can connect ~131 k GPUs with fault‑tolerant link loss. It is being implemented on Nvidia ConnectX‑8, AMD Pollara/Vulcano DPUs, Broadcom Thor NICs, and switches such as Nvidia Spectrum 4/5 and Arista EOS (Tomahawk 5). ([The Next Platform – Multipath Reliable Connection (MRC) for scalable AI clusters](https://www.nextplatform.com/connect/2026/05/12/openai-microsoft-and-friends-build-a-better-more-scalable-ethernet/5239078), 12 May 2026) **[High Credibility]**  

Arista’s AI‑focused Ethernet fabrics are moving from 800 Gb/s leaf‑spine to 1.6 Tb/s ports (2027) and introducing the ESUN protocol for rack‑level dynamic bandwidth (CPC/CPO optics). Supply‑chain constraints are expected for 1‑2 years. ([The Next Platform – Arista’s AI‑focused Ethernet fabrics (scale‑out/scale‑up/scale‑across)](https://www.nextplatform.com/connect/2026/05/07/arista-rides-ai-scale-out-networks-moves-into-scale-across-and-awaits-scale-up/5235293), 7 May 2026) **[High Credibility]**  

**Operational Impact**  
- Re‑evaluate fabric design: two‑tier high‑radix Ethernet may replace three‑tier InfiniBand.  
- Procure 100 Gb/s NICs/DPUs with MRC firmware; update switch OS for SRv6 and ECN.  
- Adjust monitoring (Prometheus exporters) to capture packet‑trimming and ECN counters.  
- Plan for higher link count (cabling, power) despite reduced switch count.  
- Begin capacity planning for 1.6 Tb/s ports; secure early ASIC orders to mitigate lead‑time risk.

### 4. GPU Hardware Evolution & Air‑Cooled Options  
AMD’s MI350P is a PCIe air‑cooled GPU with 12 HBM3E stacks, 4 TB/s bandwidth (90 % delivered), 600 W TDP (450 W throttled), delivering 58‑66 % peak FP16/FP8 compute. It is targeted at 200‑250 B parameter inference and small‑scale training, with no multi‑GPU or CPU‑GPU coherency. OEMs include Dell, HPE, Lenovo, Cisco, and Supermicro. ([The Next Platform – “Sometimes, Air Is The Only Way For AI Systems To Keep Their Cool”](https://www.nextplatform.com/compute/2026/05/08/sometimes-air-is-the-only-way-for-ai-systems-to-keep-their-cool/5237421), 8 May 2026) **[High Credibility]**  

**Operational Impact**  
- Deploy high‑performance AI nodes in air‑cooled data centers.  
- Adjust power budgeting (450 W mode saves ~25 % power).  
- Schedule single‑node inference jobs; avoid multi‑GPU coherency‑dependent workloads.  
- Verify driver/stack support for OCP‑FP8, MXFP6/4.

GA is Q3 2026. ([The Next Platform – HPE throws VM users a lifeline (Private Cloud 4)](https://www.nextplatform.com/cloud/2026/05/13/hpe-throws-vm-users-a-lifeline-unifying-containers-and-vm-management-in-cloud-stack/5239635), 13 May 2026) **[High Credibility]**  

**Operational Impact**  
- Consolidate scheduler interfaces (Slurm ↔ Morpheus API).  
- Leverage Zerto for low‑downtime migration from VMware.  
- Exploit RDMA‑enabled storage for AI data pipelines.  
- Reduce VM licensing costs (~10×).

- Upgrade power, UPS, and HVAC capacity to match higher density.  
- Accelerate refresh cycles; automate inventory/firmware management.
